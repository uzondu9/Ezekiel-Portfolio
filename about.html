Personal Journey

From childhood, I wanted to join the Nigerian military, especially the Nigerian Navy. That ambition shaped my focus throughout secondary school and beyond.

After secondary school, I applied to the Nigerian Defence Academy four times hoping to become a naval officer, building patience, readiness, and self-discipline along the way.

When I did not make the final selection list, I redirected that dedication toward another field built on long-term learning. Discovering Miva Open University and enrolling in Computer Science opened a fresh chapter focused on steady growth.
